## Goals

SpaceAero is a multi-year rocketry project with one rule: every flight should teach me something I can measure. Before building anything, I set three goals: fly rockets that collect real data, compare that data against predictions, and document everything openly, including the parts that don't go to plan.

## Tools and setup

I registered spaceaero.org and built this site to keep a running record of the project. For design and prediction I'm using OpenRocket, a free simulation tool that estimates altitude, stability, and acceleration before a flight. I keep a paper notebook for predictions and field notes so I can't adjust them after the fact.

## Safety and community

All flights follow the [NAR Model Rocket Safety Code](https://www.nar.org/safety-information/model-rocket-safety-code/): certified motors, proper launch distances, and launching only in suitable weather and open fields. I'm connecting with CENJARS (NAR Section #698), the local rocketry club, to learn from experienced flyers and launch at organized events.
